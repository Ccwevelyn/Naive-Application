Naive Application

    怎么操作

    1. 先勾地区。页面一开始是 0 所。点一次是选上，再点一次取消。地区标题旁边的「全选」在全部选中后会变成「全不选」。
    2. 搜索框认中文名，也认英文名。
    3. 专业下拉选了一门之后，综合排名里会多出一根 QS 学科拉杆。不选专业，这一项不参加。
    4. 左边五项拉杆是综合排名、实验室资源、产业连接、环境、学费。拉杆上的数字是它在综合分里占的百分比，五项加起来不能超过 100。没拉到的部分空着，不补进综合分。
    5. 综合排名下面的央国企、能源、互联网大厂、外企，只会改 QS、THE、U.S. News 三根拉杆。自己拖这三根，这四个选项会取消。
    6. 环境要先勾想去的地点类型。学校对上是 100 分，对不上是 40 分。没标注地点类型就跳过。
    7. 学费要先填一个最高学费，单位是万元/年。公布学费不超过这个数，分数是（最高学费 − 学费）÷ 最高学费 × 100。超过则是 0。没填最高学费，这一项不算。
    8. 点学校卡片中间的箭头，可以看每一项怎么加出来。页面左上方可以在中文和 English 之间切换。

    数据从哪来，分数怎么加

    这是一份选校草稿，用来按你自己的权重排一个顺序。它不是官方排名，也不代替任何学校的录取结果。某一项没有数字，这一项就跳过，它的权重也不分给别的项。

    名次来自三份世界大学排名，再加上 QS 的学科排名：

    • QS 世界大学排名。这里收入有确切名次的学校，到第 697 名。701–710 起的区间榜没有放进来。中外合办单独一列，多数没有世界排名。
    • 泰晤士高等教育世界大学排名。名次、科研环境、产业连接都来自这里。后面大量学校只公布区间，页面用的是区间起点。
    • U.S. News 世界大学排名。
    • QS 学科用的是这门学科榜自己的序号，目前有计算机、电气与电子、商业与管理、经济学、数学、物理与天文、化学、医学、生物科学。

    四份名次用同一张台阶表。卡片角标是公布名次，点开之后那一行是换算后的分数。台阶在 50、100、150、200、500。同一档里只轻轻变化，跨过一道线才掉一大截。第 1 名是 100 分，第 1000 名及以后是 0 分。

    How to use it

    1. Tick a region first. The page starts at 0 schools. One click includes that region, another click removes it. All becomes None once every region is on.
    2. Search matches the Chinese name and the English name.
    3. Choosing a subject adds a QS subject slider inside Ranking. With no subject, that piece stays out.
    4. The five sliders are Ranking, Labs, Industry links, Setting, and Tuition. The number is that item’s share of the result. The five cannot add past 100. Unused room stays unused.
    5. State-owned, Energy, Big tech, and Foreign firms only reset the QS, THE, and U.S. News mix. Dragging one of those three clears the four chips.
    6. Setting needs a campus type first. A match scores 100. A miss scores 40. An unlabeled campus is skipped.
    7. Tuition needs a highest tuition, in 10,000 CNY per year. At or under the cap, the score is (cap − tuition) / cap × 100. Over the cap, the score is 0. With no cap, tuition is skipped.
    8. The arrow on a card opens the breakdown. The language switch sits at the top left of the page.

    Sources and scoring

    This is a shortlist you weight yourself. It is not an official ranking, and it is not an admissions decision. A missing number is skipped. Its weight is not given to the other items.

    Ranks come from three world lists, plus a QS subject list:

    • QS World University Rankings. Schools with an exact rank through 697 are included. Bands from 701–710 are left out. Joint programs are a separate region, and most have no world rank.
    • Times Higher Education World University Rankings. Rank, research environment, and industry come from here. Many later places are published as a band, and this page uses the start of that band.
    • U.S. News Best Global Universities.
    • A QS subject rank is that subject list’s own number. The subjects are Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Business and Management, Economics, Mathematics, Physics and Astronomy, Chemistry, Medicine, and Biological Sciences.

    All four ranks use one step table. The badge on a card is the published rank. The breakdown shows the converted score. The steps are 50, 100, 150, 200, and 500. Inside a step the score moves a little. Crossing a step drops it. Rank 1 scores 100. Rank 1000 and after score 0.

    The steps follow published talent and settlement notices:

    • Shanghai HRSS, 8 June 2022: top 50 and 51–100 were different settlement paths. That measure ran through June 2023.
    • Lianyungang SASAC, 2026 municipal SOE hiring: QS world top 100.
    • Shenzhen Nanshan HR bureau, 28 April 2025: any one of four rankings in the top 150.
    • Fuzhou HRSS, 24 February 2026: ARWU, QS, or THE top 200.
    • Shanghai talent rule, 2025 sets a high-level university at the top 500. The Haidian youth work-residence guide uses the same line.

    Those notices also name ARWU. This page has no ARWU ranks, so ARWU is not scored. The full weight math is in the README.

    Labs use the THE research-environment score and the QS faculty/student score. Industry links use the THE Industry score. Those three are already scores from 0 to 100, so they are not run through the rank steps.

    Tuition comes from StudyPath (doi:10.5281/zenodo.22979730, CC BY 4.0). It is the cheapest annualizable international fee StudyPath recorded for that university. It can be an undergraduate course, and it can be 0. It is not one master’s invoice. Dollars use the Frankfurter rate on 20 November 2026: 1 USD = 6.6777 CNY. Housing and living costs are not part of this item.

    Campus type is a label on this page, so you can match a college town, a city, a city centre, a district, or the countryside. It is not a published environment index. An unlabeled school skips Setting.
